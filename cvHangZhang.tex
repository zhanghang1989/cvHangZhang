\begin{rSection}{About Me}
I am Hang Zhang, Head of the VLA Foundation Model Team at XPeng, where I lead the development of \href{https://www.xpeng.com/pressroom/news/019cae5e67b99c0960ee8a028129016a}{XPeng VLA 2.0}, the pure-vision, end-to-end Vision-Language-Action foundation model powering XPeng's production driver-assistance system across 300+ cities in China, as well as its overseas vehicles and Robotaxi fleet. I designed \href{https://pandaily.com/xpeng-second-gen-vla-time}{Infini VLA}, an industry-first streaming VLA architecture that entered production in 2026. I was recognized as one of the \href{https://www.163.com/dy/article/KK6K284605278VAD.html}{Top 50 Intelligent Driving Developers in China (2025)}.

Previously, I was a Senior Staff Applied Research Scientist at Cruise, where I led the large-scale perception model consolidation effort across detection, segmentation, and 3D perception systems for autonomous driving.

Before Cruise, I was a Research Scientist at Meta, leading the development of a scalable neural architecture optimization platform supporting AI models across Instagram, Portal, and VR devices for applications including person understanding, AR/VR rendering, and ads ranking.

Earlier, I was a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon AI, working on deep learning and computer vision systems and contributing to the MXNet ecosystem. During that time, we developed ResNeSt, which achieved state-of-the-art results on multiple computer vision benchmarks.

My research has received 12,000+ citations on Google Scholar, and my open-source projects have accumulated 10,000+ GitHub stars.

More about me: [\href{https://hangzhang.org/}{Homepage}] [\href{https://github.com/zhanghang1989}{GitHub}] [\href{https://www.linkedin.com/in/zhanghang0704}{LinkedIn}] [\href{https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gCoWdkUAAAAJ}{Google Scholar}]
\end{rSection}

\begin{rSubsection}{XPeng}{\em Dec 2024 - Now}{Senior Principal Engineer | Senior Director, Head of VLA Foundation Model}{Silicon Valley, CA}

\item Head of the VLA Foundation Model team ($\sim$45 researchers and engineers across Silicon Valley and China) building \href{https://www.xpeng.com/pressroom/news/019cae5e67b99c0960ee8a028129016a}{XPeng VLA 2.0}, the pure-vision, end-to-end model behind XPeng's production driver-assistance system in 300+ Chinese cities, overseas vehicles, and Robotaxi; took it from research to production in about a year.

\item Designed and led \textbf{\href{https://pandaily.com/xpeng-second-gen-vla-time}{Infini VLA}}, an industry-first streaming VLA architecture shipped in 2026: LLM-style long-sequence training with autoregressive streaming inference over a temporal KV cache, decoupling long-horizon memory from transient reaction on unchanged on-vehicle compute. Wrote the initial implementation and led training, optimization, and integration.

\item Delivered three consecutive 2026 releases that roughly halved key safety and intervention metrics across highway, urban, and parking scenarios while raising VLA mileage penetration by over 50\%.
