\begin{rSection}{Experience}
\begin{rSubsection}{XPeng}{Dec 2024 -- Present}{Senior Principal Engineer / Senior Director}{Silicon Valley, CA}
\item[] \textbf{Head of VLA Foundation Model}
\item Lead approximately \textbf{45 researchers and engineers} across Silicon Valley and China developing vision-language-action foundation models, spanning architecture, training, optimization, and production integration.
\item Led \href{https://www.xpeng.com/pressroom/news/019cae5e67b99c0960ee8a028129016a}{\textbf{XPeng VLA 2.0}} from research to production in approximately one year. The pure-vision, end-to-end model powers driver assistance across \textbf{300+ Chinese cities}, overseas vehicles, and Robotaxi.
\item Delivered three consecutive 2026 releases that roughly \textbf{halved key safety and intervention metrics} across highway, urban, and parking scenarios, while increasing VLA mileage penetration by \textbf{over 50\%}.
\item Designed \href{https://pandaily.com/xpeng-second-gen-vla-time}{\textbf{Infini VLA}}, a streaming VLA architecture shipped in 2026. Combined LLM-style long-sequence training with \textbf{autoregressive streaming inference over a temporal KV cache}, separating long-horizon visual memory from immediate reaction on unchanged on-vehicle compute. \textbf{Wrote the initial implementation} and led training, optimization, and integration.
\item Advised \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30567}{\textbf{TuringLLM}}, a 20B-A2B mixture-of-experts VLM for physical AI, and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24253}{\textbf{TuringViT}}, advancing the team's language and vision foundation models.
\item Advised \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28758}{\textbf{X-Mind}}, a predictive world model using visual prediction as visual chain-of-thought for end-to-end driving.
\end{rSubsection}
